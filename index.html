    Privacy Policy

    Magos Burgers & Shakes · Last updated 31 October 2026

    In short: this app keeps your cart on your own phone, and your order goes to us through WhatsApp. We do not run a customer database and we do not sell your information.

    1. Who is responsible

    Magos Burgers & Shakes, Millenium Mall, G. Silundika Street, between 5th & 6th Avenue, Bulawayo, Zimbabwe. Phone / WhatsApp: 078 081 4597 or 073 865 210.

    2. What we collect

    We only ask for what we need to prepare and deliver your order:

    • Your name — so we know who the order is for.
    • Your phone / WhatsApp number — so we can confirm the order and reach you about it.
    • Order details — the items, quantities, total, your chosen date and time, and whether it is takeaway, seat-in or delivery.
    • Delivery address — only if you choose delivery.
    • Notes — anything you type in the notes box, such as “no onions” or an allergy warning.

    We do not collect payment card details, bank details, your date of birth, your location, or any advertising identifier. The app has no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising.

    3. How your information is used

    • To receive, confirm, prepare and hand over your order.
    • To contact you about that order — for example to confirm the total, the delivery fee or a change in availability.
    • To deal with a complaint, refund or correction if something goes wrong.

    We do not use your details for marketing, and we do not send you promotional messages unless you separately ask us to.

    4. Where your information is stored

    This is the important part, and it is deliberately simple:

    • On your device. Your cart, your last order and the name, phone number and address you last used are saved in your browser’s local storage on your own phone or computer. This is what lets the app work offline and remember your details next time. This data never leaves your device unless you send an order.
    • In WhatsApp. When you press send, your order becomes a WhatsApp message. It is then held by WhatsApp under WhatsApp’s own privacy terms, and by us in our WhatsApp Business chat history.
    • We do not operate a customer database. This app has no server of its own and no account system. We do not upload your cart or your details anywhere.

    5. Third-party services

    • WhatsApp (Meta). Orders are delivered through WhatsApp. Your use of WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp’s privacy policy, which we do not control. If you would rather not use WhatsApp, phone us on 078 081 4597 and order by voice.
    • Google Maps. The “Get directions” link opens Google Maps in a new tab. We do not embed a map, so Google does not receive anything from you until you choose to tap that link. Once you do, Google’s privacy policy applies.
    • Your device and browser. The app is served as a normal web page and can be installed to your home screen. Your browser or operating system may keep its own technical logs, which we do not access.

    6. How long we keep it (data retention)

    • On your device: until you clear it. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, or by using the app’s own clear option. Uninstalling the app removes it too.
    • In WhatsApp: for as long as we need the chat to handle your order and any follow-up, and then for a reasonable period for record-keeping. You can delete the chat from your own side at any time.
    • We do not keep your details anywhere else, so there is nothing else to retain.

    7. Who we share it with

    We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share it only with:

    • Our own staff who prepare and deliver your order.
    • A delivery rider, where one is used — limited to your name, phone number, address and the order itself.
    • WhatsApp, as the channel that carries the message.
    • A authority, if we are legally required to disclose it.

    8. Your rights

    You may ask us at any time to:

    • tell you what information we hold about you;
    • correct anything that is wrong;
    • delete the order history we hold in WhatsApp;
    • stop contacting you.

    Message or call us on 078 081 4597 and we will deal with it. Because we hold no database, most requests can be met immediately. You can also clear everything stored on your own device yourself, without asking us.

    9. Keeping your information safe

    Because the app stores your details only on your own device, the main protection is your device’s own lock screen and your WhatsApp account security. Please keep your phone locked and do not place orders from a shared or public device if you would rather your name and number were not saved there.
